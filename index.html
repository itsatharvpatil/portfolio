About

I'm drawn to the same kind of problem: something has to work safely, right now, with thin evidence to go on. On a ventilator, that meant reading a premature baby's breath through a noisy sensor and deciding whether to intervene, in under 8 milliseconds, validated on real neonatal data. Now it's reading the surface of a metal plate on a production line and deciding whether it's defective before it ships.

At Fraunhofer IWU I work on the data side of that problem. There usually aren't enough real defective plates to train a detector properly, so I generate synthetic ones instead — photorealistic enough to stand in for the real thing. How far that substitution actually holds up is what my Master's thesis is about.

Generative AI and Computer Vision – HiWi

Fraunhofer IWU, Prüftechnik und Automation, Chemnitz

Jun 2026 – Present

Defect detectors need examples of defects. A well-run production line barely produces any — so the training data for catching failures is exactly the data you don't have.

I build them instead. An end-to-end pipeline composites synthetic defects onto real metal surfaces with zone-based blending and TPS warping, and a LoRA-fine-tuned Stable Diffusion handles the edits that need to look photorealistic rather than pasted. A PatchCore evaluation loop then answers the question that actually matters: does higher visual fidelity make the defect easier for a downstream model to catch?

Biomedical Signal Processing & Data Science — Werkstudent

Ingenieurbüro: Dr.-Ing. Eugen Koch, Braunschweig

Nov 2023 – Jan 2026

A premature baby's breath is a faint, noisy signal. A ventilator that mistimes its support fights the patient instead of helping — and it has milliseconds to decide.

I built the detection layer: Savitzky-Golay filtering with adaptive normalization to pull the breath out of the noise, wrapped in a hybrid control algorithm with a fail-safe apnea state machine, so an ambiguous reading degrades safely rather than triggering wrongly. Thresholds were tuned with Azure ML HyperDrive and watched live through a PyQtGraph diagnostics dashboard.
