ABOUT

Adaptive by method.

My work spans industrial AI, biomedical signal processing, computer vision, generative AI, time-series modelling, data engineering and IT systems.

The subject changes. The way I approach it does not. I start with the real constraint, build a baseline, test alternatives, and keep the metric tied to the actual decision the system has to make.

That mindset is what lets me move from a noisy respiratory signal to a low-latency trigger, from scarce industrial defect data to synthetic examples, or from a large flight dataset to a memory-efficient prediction pipeline.
